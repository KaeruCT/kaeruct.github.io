## Official Setup

I think it's important to allow engineers to tweak with their own AI coding setup. Most engineers are natural tinkerers and we enjoy trying out different tools and see what works best. My suggestion is to provide an "official", "golden-path" set of tools and configurations, but allow for extension whenever possible.

And of course, don't restrict what the engineers are allowed to use. Simply let them know that if they're using something "unsupported" and they need help, they're on their own. It's very valuable to allow people to experiment, maybe they find something better to what's officially supported, and then the official setup can be improved for everyone.
